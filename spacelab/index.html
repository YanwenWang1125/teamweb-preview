Three questions we work on

Intelligence

How can learning run efficiently across many devices when every model update has to cross a wireless link?

AI for networks, and networks for distributed intelligence.

Entanglement

How can large combinatorial problems fit on today’s qubit-limited quantum hardware without giving up solution quality?

Quantum optimization for problems in communication systems.

Connectivity

How should antennas, surfaces, power, and access be configured so that many users share a channel efficiently and securely?

Wireless foundations, flexible radio environments, and trustworthy links.

Selected research

Qubit-Efficient Quantum Computing

A QAOA framework that encodes a problem’s symmetry-constrained subspace with fewer qubits, aiming to keep the original optimization performance on qubit-limited hardware.

Read the paper (arXiv)

Communication-Efficient Edge AI

Jointly choosing which devices join federated learning and how they share NOMA radio resources, to cut the cost of training over wireless links.

Read the paper (IEEE Xplore)

Pinching Antenna Systems

A low-complexity method for placing pinching antennas along a waveguide, bringing each antenna close to the users it serves.

Read the paper (IEEE Xplore)
