    Qubit-Efficient Quantum Computing

    A QAOA framework that encodes a problem’s symmetry-constrained subspace with fewer qubits, aiming to keep the original optimization performance on qubit-limited hardware.

    Communication-Efficient Edge AI

    Jointly choosing which devices join federated learning and how they share NOMA radio resources, to cut the cost of training over wireless links.
